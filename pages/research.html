Core Disciplines

Research Areas

BPS members conduct research spanning the full breadth of phytopathological science.

Mycology

Study of fungal plant pathogens and disease mechanisms.

Bacteriology

Investigating bacterial diseases affecting major crops.

Virology

Understanding plant viruses and vector transmission.

Nematology

Research on plant-parasitic nematodes and their control.

Phytoplasmology

Study of phytoplasma-associated plant diseases.

Disease Epidemiology

Understanding disease spread patterns and forecasting.

Disease Diagnosis

Modern diagnostic techniques for accurate pathogen ID.

Integrated Disease Management

Combining strategies for sustainable disease control.

Biotechnology

Biotech tools applied to plant disease resistance.

Molecular Plant Pathology

Molecular basis of plant-pathogen interactions.

Plant-Microbe Interaction

Exploring beneficial and harmful microbial relationships.

Sustainable Crop Protection

Eco-friendly approaches to long-term crop health.
