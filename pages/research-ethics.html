Research Ethics

Our Commitment

Standards of Research Integrity

BPS is committed to promoting the highest standards of research ethics among its members, ensuring credible, reproducible and responsibly conducted science.

Data integrity and transparent, honest reporting
Fair authorship credit and acknowledgment practices
Compliance with biosafety and biosecurity regulations
Avoidance of plagiarism and data fabrication
Conflict of interest disclosure in publications
Environmentally responsible experimental practices
Full research ethics policy document to be published by BPS Administration.
