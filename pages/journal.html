About BJPP

Advancing Plant Pathology Knowledge

The Bangladesh Journal of Plant Pathology (BJPP) publishes original research articles, review papers and short communications spanning mycology, bacteriology, virology, nematology, phytoplasmology and integrated disease management.

  • Rigorous double-blind peer review
  • Published periodically for members and subscribers
  • Open to submissions from national and international researchers
