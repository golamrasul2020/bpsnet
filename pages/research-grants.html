About BPS Research Grants

BPS periodically offers small research grants to support member-led studies on plant disease diagnosis, management and epidemiology, prioritizing projects with practical impact on Bangladeshi agriculture.

Grant amounts, deadlines and eligibility criteria to be confirmed and published by BPS Administration.
Eligibility
  • Active BPS members in good standing
  • Research proposal aligned with BPS priority areas
  • Institutional affiliation with a recognized organization
Application Process
Submit proposal & budget via application form
Review by BPS Research Committee
