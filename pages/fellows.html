Fellows of BPS

Honoring outstanding contributions to plant pathology in Bangladesh.

Distinction

BPS Fellowship

Fellowship is the Society's highest form of recognition, granted by nomination to scientists who have made exceptional contributions to phytopathological research, education or professional service in Bangladesh.

The official list of BPS Fellows will be published here once confirmed by BPS Administration.
Nomination

How Fellowship Works

Active members nominate candidates with distinguished records
Advisory Board reviews nominations for merit
Fellowship conferred at the Biennial Conference
