Who We Are

A Professional Home for Plant Pathologists

The Bangladesh Phytopathological Society (BPS) is a nonprofit, science-based professional society bringing together researchers, academics, extension specialists and students dedicated to the study and management of plant diseases in Bangladesh.

BPS covers the full breadth of phytopathological science, including:

  • Mycology — the study of fungal plant pathogens
  • Bacteriology — bacterial disease research
  • Virology — plant viruses and vector transmission
  • Nematology — plant-parasitic nematodes
  • Phytoplasmology — phytoplasma-associated diseases
  • Plant disease management & crop protection
Our History

The Bangladesh Phytopathological Society was established to unite the country's plant pathologists under a single professional platform. Since its founding, BPS has grown into a recognized voice for phytopathological science in Bangladesh.

Our Objectives
  • Advance phytopathological science in Bangladesh
  • Promote plant disease research and diagnosis
  • Organize conferences, symposia and scientific events
  • Support crop protection and sustainable agriculture
  • Promote farmer awareness of plant disease management
  • Support research grants and scientific publishing
  • Promote Bangladeshi plant pathologists internationally
  • Develop international scientific linkages and collaboration
Professional Activities

BPS organizes a range of professional activities throughout the year, including biennial conferences, seminars, training workshops, and publication of the Bangladesh Journal of Plant Pathology.
