Vision & Mission

The guiding principles that shape everything BPS does.

Our Vision

"Creating an environment for growing healthy plants for a sustainable future."

BPS envisions a Bangladesh where scientific knowledge of plant health directly supports thriving farms, resilient ecosystems and long-term food security.

Our Mission

"Engaging plant pathology professionals to advance plant health through innovative and environmentally sustainable practices."

BPS achieves this mission by connecting researchers, funding scientific dialogue, and translating research into practical, farmer-accessible disease management solutions.

Objectives

What BPS Strives to Achieve

Nine core objectives guide the Society's programs, publications and partnerships.

Advance phytopathological science in Bangladesh
Promote plant disease research and applied diagnosis
Organize conferences, symposia and scientific events
Support crop protection through science-based methods
Promote farmer awareness of disease management
Facilitate research grants for member scientists
Publish scientific information and journals
Promote Bangladeshi plant pathologists internationally
Develop international scientific linkages
